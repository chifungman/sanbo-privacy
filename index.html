Privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026

Account

Sanbo signs you in with your email. That address is sent to Supabase, which stores the account and emails you a sign-in link. We do not sell email addresses or use them for ads.

How long we keep the account

Supabase keeps the account until you delete it. Signing out only removes the session on this phone. It does not delete the account.

Deleting the account

In Settings, open Account, choose Delete account, and confirm. That deletes the Supabase user, the public dog card, and the sticker stored for that account. Photos and routes stay on this phone. Delete those patrols, or delete the app, to remove them. We do not keep a copy.

Photos and routes

Photos and patrol routes stay on this phone. GPS points and an exact home stay on this phone. We do not upload them. The profile photo, sex, birth date, and Instagram handle stay on this phone too.

Public dog card

If you are signed in, Sanbo may save a dog card to Supabase: name, username, breed, home district, a sticker image, who can see the card, and totals such as distance and patrol count. The district is not a street address. Only you is stored and not listed. Everyone and Followers can be read by other signed-in people. Anyone with the sticker link can load that image.

Maps

When a map is on screen, that view sends your location to Mapbox so the map can be drawn. We do not use location for ads or to track you across apps from other companies. Sanbo does not use advertising or analytics. Mapbox describes what it does with map data at https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy.

While you patrol

Sanbo uses your location to draw the patrol. Background location runs only while a patrol is recording, and the blue status bar stays on. It stops when the patrol stops. The route is saved on this phone.
